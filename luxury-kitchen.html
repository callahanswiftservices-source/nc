Sculptural Function

Book-Matched Marble & Fluted American Walnut

Designed for an avid collector and culinary entertainer, this Upper East Side kitchen features a monolithic 12-foot waterfall island carved from single-quarry Calacatta Viola marble.

Behind hidden walnut pocket doors lies a concealed secondary prep kitchen, wine room, and espresso bar with automated touch sensors.
