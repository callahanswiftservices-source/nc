Heritage of Excellence

Redefining Architectural Aesthetics Since 2004

Design MVN STUDIO was founded on a singular conviction: that environment shapes emotion, intellect, and wellbeing.

Over the past two decades, our Manhattan-based studio has grown into a globally recognized architectural practice. We unite master architects, interior curators, lighting consultants, and custom furniture craftspeople under one roof.

Whether crafting a waterfront estate in the Hamptons or re-imagining a duplex penthouse in Manhattan, our approach balances understated restraint with tactile luxury.

Core Principles

Mission, Vision & Core Values

Our Mission

To design spatially harmonious, structurally pristine environments that enrich the lives of our clients while setting new benchmarks in luxury architectural craftsmanship.

Our Vision

To remain the definitive international authority in bespoke architectural interior design, recognized for timeless aesthetics that outlast transient trends.

Core Values

Material Integrity, Architectural Restraint, Uncompromising Precision, Transparent Collaboration, and Sustainable Innovation.
