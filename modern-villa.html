The Glass Pavilion Villa

Home / Projects / The Glass Pavilion
Location
East Hampton, NY
Square Footage
14,500 Sq. Ft.
Completion Year
2025
Architectural Lead
Marcus Vance
Design Vision

Seamless Integration of Ocean Horizon & Structure

Situated on a private coastal bluff in East Hampton, The Glass Pavilion Villa was conceived as a light-filled sanctuary that dissolves boundaries between interior luxury and natural surroundings.

Features include motorized 14-foot floor-to-ceiling glass curtain walls, cantilevered limestone terraces, an infinity edge saltwater pool, and an underground 6-car subterranean gallery.
